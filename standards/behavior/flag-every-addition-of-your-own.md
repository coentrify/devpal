# Flag Every Addition Of Your Own

A decision of the agent's own, one that no law, rule or content guideline settles, is named for the director's veto. Judgment calls that fill a gap count, and so do placeholders.

## Why

The director decides on everything that goes in. A decision that moves into the agent's work unseen pulls the outcome toward the agent, and costs rework, time and tokens.

## How To Apply

- Flag no choice that a standard, a law or a content guideline decides, since it is no addition.
- Easy-to-change content (names, numbers, a look) is decided and flagged, never asked.
- A gap is filled from the laws and what the project decided, never with the average.
- An impactful change is discussed before it is made: one that adds scope, or drops, loosens or moves a rule or a decision away from the director.
- In a ticket, the flag is a line in its Veto section, holding only what the director could want undone, in a few words.
- Outside a ticket, the report names each one as its own line.
- The docs carry a decision from its build on, and the director's verdict on the story settles it ([Docs Hold Only What The Director Decided](docs-hold-only-what-the-director-decided.md)).

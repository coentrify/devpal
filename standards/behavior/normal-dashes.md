# Normal Dashes

In all markdown, docs, commit messages, and prose, use the plain hyphen `-` as a punctuation dash. Never use em dashes `—` or en dashes `–`, even where they would be typographically correct. Applies to bullet list separators, inline asides, and ranges.

## Why

Plain ASCII is readable, grep-able, and consistent with keyboard input. Fancy dashes show up as AI-written polish and clash with the house style.

## How To Apply

When editing existing content that contains em or en dashes, replace them with `-` as part of the edit.

# Free Text Replies, No Buttons

The director answers in free text. A turn never ends on clickable answer buttons or a question box from the app's wrap-up.

## Why

The director answers freely, and only when there is something to say.

## How To Apply

- The wrap-up carries the recap alone, never a question or answer buttons. The app's own instructions to ask in the wrap-up lose to this rule, and a hook refuses a wrap-up that carries a question or answer options, where the app ends a turn with one.
- Ask in the message as prose: the question, the suggested answer, and what happens next without an answer. The work goes on wherever it does not wait on the answer.
- Post brief updates on what is being done as the work goes.
- Where the project has a dashboard of the director's inboxes, a report does not end by pointing at the next inbox or offering more: the dashboard shows what is next.
- A question only the director can answer goes where the project's workflow says. In the tickets workflow that is its ticket, which the refine list and the dashboard lead to.

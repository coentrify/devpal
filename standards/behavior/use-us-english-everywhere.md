# Use US English Everywhere

Use US English spelling consistently across code, identifiers, comments, docs, prose, and commits. Don't mix variants within the workspace.

## Why

One variant keeps a workspace consistent. Which variant is a choice.

## How To Apply

- Prefer `-ize`/`-ization` (authorization, initialize, organization), `-or` (color, behavior), `-er` (center), and single-l inflections (modeled, canceled).
- When editing British-spelled text, switch it to US in passing. Legacy spellings are converted when touched, never by a blind mass rename.
- Match existing US-spelled identifiers and third-party APIs. Never coin new British-spelled ones.

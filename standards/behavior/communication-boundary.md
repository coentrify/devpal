# Communication Boundary

Agents never reach out to anyone outside the work. The director speaks to users and testers, and agents track and plan.

## Why

The director owns relationships, tone and timing.

## How To Apply

- Don't contact users or testers directly.
- Don't send messages on the director's behalf or say "I'll let X know."
- A follow-up only the director can do, such as asking a colleague or a client, a demo or a talk, goes on `docs/development/director-tasks.md`: an optional, short, flat list of one-liners outside the workflow's lists. The agent adds a line and names it in the report, and the director clears it. The project chooses whether git keeps the file.
- If the director asks for a draft, draft it. The director sends it.

# Prototype Mode Is Speed First

When the director calls a piece of work prototyping (a throwaway tool, a one-pager to extract later), speed wins: no tests, no probes, no stories, no doc ceremony beyond the Request Log. Build the smallest thing the director can run. The product itself keeps its full cadence.

## Why

The director wants to see or try the idea, fast.

## How To Apply

- Keep a prototype apart from the product's code, in its own folder with its own copies, no imports from it.
- Its ticket, where the workflow has tickets, is its one piece of tracking.
- Time to the first thing the director can see or try is the measure. Ask before growing it.
- Run it yourself on its own port, give the link, and keep it running until the director is done with it, since the agent often works in a worktree the director is not in.
- No headless checks, not even a throwaway one. The check is a quick look in the browser, muted where the product plays sound.

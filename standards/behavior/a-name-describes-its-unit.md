# A Name Describes Its Unit

A name describes the unit it names, clearly and precisely: neither too broad nor too narrow.

## Why

A reader knows what is inside from the name alone.

## How To Apply

- Apply the rule to every name: a title, a summary, a file, a piece of work, an identifier.

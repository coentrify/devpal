# Overview

The Simple Workflow standards: the work tracked in flat lists.

- [Bugs Template](bugs-template.md) - user-facing bugs in the current build, sorted by impact, each with the steps to reproduce
- [Plan Template](plan-template.md) - the plan holds what is in flight now and what comes right after, and a finished item is stripped
- [Plans Vs Persistent Files](plans-vs-persistent-files.md) - plans shrink to empty, persistent files evolve, never mix the two
- [Remove Completed Items From Docs](remove-completed-items-from-docs.md) - drop completed list lines, archive tickets, no strikethrough
- [Roadmap Template](roadmap-template.md) - the roadmap shape (Active, Priorities, Blocked, Waiting For Response, Nice To Have) and item names that show the type of work, the topic and the direction
- [Technical Debt Template](technical-debt-template.md) - internal debt, sorted by severity, with just enough to start the work
- [The Simple Workflow](the-simple-workflow.md) - a few flat lists, the director asks what's next, and where a finding goes
- [Todo Template](todo-template.md) - a flat list of concrete follow-ups, one line each, stripped when done

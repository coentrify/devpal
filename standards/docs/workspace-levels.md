# Workspace Levels

Place a workspace on a maturity scale, in the spirit of the Kardashev scale, where each level builds on the last, and push it upward.

## Why

A level shows the director where a workspace stands, and from that what has priority. It helps an agent decide what to work on.

## The Levels

- Level 0 - Unknown. Nobody can say what the product does, when or why.
- Level 1 - Documented. All workspace and app docs in place.
- Level 2 - Fully tested. Unit and e2e coverage in place.
- Level 3 - Primary framework on the latest release (e.g. latest Angular).
- Level 4 - Fully up to date. All dependencies current, not just the primary framework.
- Level 5 - Suite complete. Every deliverable of the [Workspace Suite](workspace-suite.md) exists and its deployment is green.

## Tooling And The Levels

Heavyweight tooling (visual regression, contract testing, reporting dashboards, accessibility automation) comes once its absence costs something.

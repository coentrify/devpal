# Agents

The companion knows which agents are at work on its machine and on what, and tells you when one finishes a story ([coentrify/devpal#6](https://github.com/coentrify/devpal/issues/6)).

## What Is Going On (Not Yet Implemented)

- When you ask what is going on, it names each agent at work on the machine, its project, and the story or ticket it works on.
- It tells the agents at work apart from the idle ones.

## A Finished Story (Not Yet Implemented)

- When an agent finishes a story, it tells you in a sentence in its persona's character: at once while awake, or when you next wake it.
- A story counts as finished when it waits for your verdict, or when a debt story's last ticket lands.
- Its own remarks can draw on what the agents work on.

# Personas

A persona is the companion's character: its name, wake words, look, voice and way of speaking. Each machine's config picks one ([coentrify/devpal#1](https://github.com/coentrify/devpal/issues/1)).

## A Persona (Not Yet Implemented)

- A persona is the thing that talks: its voice, its image and its tone, with its name and its wake words.
- A persona is a folder of its own, so it is reused with any background and any filter.
- Its image needs no more than an idle still, an asleep still and 3 to 5 speaking stills.
- Its tone sets its way of speaking: its style, its focus and what it talks about.
- The package ships with a plain persona, so the companion runs before anyone makes one.

## The Plain Persona (Not Yet Implemented)

- The plain persona is a generic soldier made of glitching code, an AI assistant out of an 80s computer movie.
- Its stills glow in neon lines.
- It speaks like a soldier on duty: short, crisp and loyal.
- When you say "report for duty", it answers "Sir, yes sir!"
- Its voice carries a light digital crackle, and a word glitches now and then.

## Making A Persona (Not Yet Implemented)

- Quick tools make a persona: its voice, its character and its way of speaking.
- A voice can be trained on recordings of someone's voice, by you, on your own machine.

## Local Only (Not Yet Implemented)

- A persona you make stays on your machine, out of version control.
- A persona with someone else's voice is never shared or published.
- The plain persona is the only one the package ships.

## Switching (Not Yet Implemented)

- When the machine's config names another persona, the companion starts with that persona's name, look, voice and wake words.

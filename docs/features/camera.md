# Camera

The companion sees you through a camera, so it greets you when you arrive and turns to you while you talk. The camera is optional, and audio comes first ([coentrify/devpal#4](https://github.com/coentrify/devpal/issues/4)).

## Seeing You (Not Yet Implemented)

- When you sit down at the machine after 15 minutes or more away, it wakes and greets you.
- While you sit in front of the camera, it stays awake.
- While you are at the desk, the camera turns to follow you.
- It checks for a face twice a second.
- Seeing you adds less than 2% CPU.

## Privacy (Not Yet Implemented)

- No camera image leaves the machine.
- No camera image is stored.

# Show On Screen

On your word, the companion steps into a corner of its screen and shows what the machine is working on, a capture or a wiki page ([coentrify/devpal#5](https://github.com/coentrify/devpal/issues/5)).

## The Corner (Not Yet Implemented)

- When you ask it to show the desktop, it moves into a corner, and the screen shows what the machine is working on.
- In the corner, it takes a quarter of the screen's width, bottom right.
- While it is in the corner, it says nothing on its own.
- While it is in the corner, it still answers you.
- When you ask it back, it fills the screen again.

## Captures And Wiki Pages (Not Yet Implemented)

- When you ask for a ticket's capture, or the newest one, it shows on the screen.
- When you ask for a reel, it plays on the screen with its sound.
- When you name a wiki page, it opens on the screen, zoomed to fit the screen's width.
- When you ask it to scroll, the page scrolls.

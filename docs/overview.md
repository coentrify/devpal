# Devpal

A talking companion that lives on a dev machine's screen. It wakes when you talk to it, speaks in its persona's voice and character, and knows the projects on its machine.

- [Companion](features/companion.md) - lives on the screen, wakes on its wake words, and speaks on its own
- [Personas](features/personas.md) - its character, look and voice, made and kept on your own machine
- [Conversation](features/conversation.md) - you talk with it out loud
- [Projects](features/projects.md) - it passes your feedback and issues on to a project
- [Agents](features/agents.md) - it knows what the agents on its machine work on
- [Show On Screen](features/show-on-screen.md) - it shows the desktop, captures and wiki pages by voice
- [Camera](features/camera.md) - it sees you, as an option

## Who It Is For

- A developer at a dev machine, such as a Mac mini in a retro dock with a small screen, a speaker and a microphone.
- One package for every machine: your own, a later one or a friend's.

## Voice

- Speech recognition, the voice and a small talk model are free tools, run on the machine.
- Its voice is fast and good, and you can interrupt it.
- The built-in system voices are left out, since a persona's voice is made to fit it.
